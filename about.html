About Velnov

Velnov is an online learning platform designed to make learning simple, practical, and engaging through quizzes, practice questions, explanations, reference materials, and useful learning tools.

What You Can Find on Velnov

Velnov provides learning resources across a range of subjects and topics, including programming, science, mathematics, history, English grammar, and more.

Users can choose a subject, explore topics, take quizzes, answer practice questions, and review explanations to strengthen their understanding.

Learning Tools

In addition to quizzes, Velnov provides mathematics formulas and a scientific calculator to support learning and problem solving.

Our Approach

Velnov follows a simple approach: learn a concept, practice with questions, review the explanation, and continue improving.

The goal is to provide an easy-to-use learning environment that students, learners, and anyone interested in practicing a subject can access from their browser.
