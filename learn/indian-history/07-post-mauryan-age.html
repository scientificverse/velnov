Q1. medium

The dynasty that succeeded the Mauryas in Magadha was the:

  1. A Shunga dynasty
  2. B Gupta dynasty
  3. C Kushana dynasty
  4. D Satavahana dynasty

Answer: A — Shunga dynasty

The Shunga dynasty (c. 185-73 BCE), founded by Pushyamitra Shunga after killing the last Maurya, ruled Magadha and parts of central India, with its capital at Pataliputra (later Vidisha).

Shunga dynastyPushyamitra

  1. A The Shungas (he is associated with Pushyamitra)
  2. B The Mauryas
  3. C The Guptas
  4. D The Cholas

Answer: A — The Shungas (he is associated with Pushyamitra)

Patanjali, who wrote the Mahabhashya (a commentary on Panini's grammar), lived in the Shunga period and is associated with the court of Pushyamitra Shunga.

  1. A Indo-Greeks (Bactrian Greeks)
  2. B Kushanas
  3. C Sakas
  4. D Parthians

Answer: A — Indo-Greeks (Bactrian Greeks)

The Indo-Greeks (or Bactrian Greeks) ruled the northwest from the 2nd century BCE. They were the first to issue gold coins and coins bearing rulers' portraits and names in India.
