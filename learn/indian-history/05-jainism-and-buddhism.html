Easy questions (2)

Q1. easy

Jainism and Buddhism both arose in which century?

  1. A 6th century BCE
  2. B 3rd century CE
  3. C 10th century BCE
  4. D 1st century CE

Answer: A — 6th century BCE

Both religions arose in the 6th century BCE in the Magadha region, amid social and economic changes that produced dissatisfaction with elaborate Vedic ritualism and the caste order.

6th century BCENew religions

Q24. easy

The founder of Buddhism, Gautama Buddha, was born with which personal name?

  1. A Siddhartha
  2. B Vardhamana
  3. C Ashoka
  4. D Rahula

Answer: A — Siddhartha

The Buddha's birth name was Siddhartha ('one who has accomplished his aim'). Gautama was his gotra/family name. After enlightenment he was called 'Buddha' (the awakened one).

In Jainism, the spiritual teachers who 'ford the stream' of rebirth are called:

A Tirthankara ('ford-maker') is one who shows the way across the ocean of existence. Jain tradition holds there were 24 Tirthankaras.

Jainism recognises 24 Tirthankaras. The first was Rishabhanatha (Adinatha) and the last (24th) was Mahavira.

  1. A Parshvanatha
  2. B Rishabhanatha

  4. D Ajitanatha

Answer: A — Parshvanatha

Parshvanatha (symbol: snake) is regarded as historical. He taught four vows (Chaturyama): non-violence, truth, non-stealing and non-possession. Mahavira added a fifth.

Vardhamana Mahavira (the 24th Tirthankara) systematised and spread Jainism. Though Jainism predates him, he is regarded as its real historical founder.

Q13. medium

The supreme principle of Jainism, emphasised more strongly than in any other religion, is:

  1. A Ahimsa (non-violence toward all living beings)
  2. B Fire sacrifice
  3. C Idol worship
  4. D Pilgrimage
