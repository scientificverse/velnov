Q1. easy

The Indus Valley Civilization is also known as the:

  1. A Harappan Civilization
  2. B Vedic Civilization
  3. C Mauryan Civilization
  4. D Sangam Civilization

Answer: A — Harappan Civilization

It is called the Harappan Civilization after Harappa, the first site to be discovered (1921). It is also called the Indus-Saraswati Civilization.

HarappaBronze Age

The Harappan Civilization belongs to which age based on the metals used?

  1. A Bronze Age
  2. B Iron Age
  3. C Stone Age
  4. D Golden Age

The Harappans used bronze (copper + tin) but NOT iron - iron was unknown to them. Hence it is a Bronze Age civilization.

Q70. hard

Which of the following metals was UNKNOWN to the Harappans?

  1. A Iron
  2. B Copper
  3. C Bronze
  4. D Gold

Answer: A — Iron

The Harappans knew copper, bronze, gold, silver and lead - but NOT iron. Iron came to India only later, in the Vedic period, making the Harappan a Bronze Age civilization.
