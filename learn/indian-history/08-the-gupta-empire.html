Easy questions (1)

Q1. easy

The Gupta period is often called the 'Golden Age' of ancient India because of:

  1. A Great achievements in art, literature, science, and mathematics
  2. B The largest empire in world history
  3. C The introduction of Islam
  4. D The building of the Taj Mahal

Answer: A — Great achievements in art, literature, science, and mathematics

The Gupta age (c. 320-550 CE) saw remarkable flowering in Sanskrit literature (Kalidasa), science (Aryabhata), mathematics (concept of zero, decimals), astronomy, and temple/sculptural art - hence the 'Golden Age' label.

Golden AgeGupta period
