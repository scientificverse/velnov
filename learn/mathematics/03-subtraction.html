Q58. hard

Which overall statement about subtraction is CORRECT?

  1. A Subtraction means taking away to find what is left or the difference; we subtract ones first, borrowing from the tens when needed, and it is the opposite of addition
  2. B Subtraction always makes a bigger number
  3. C The order of numbers does not matter in subtraction
  4. D We never borrow in subtraction

Answer: A — Subtraction means taking away to find what is left or the difference; we subtract ones first, borrowing from the tens when needed, and it is the opposite of addition

This sums up the topic: subtraction finds the difference or what is left; we subtract column by column and borrow from the next place when the top digit is smaller; and it undoes addition, which is how we can check our answer.
