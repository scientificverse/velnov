Easy questions (5)

Q1. easy

In India, we count money in rupees and:

  1. A paise
  2. B dollars
  3. C kilograms
  4. D metres

Answer: A — paise

Indian money is counted in rupees (₹) and paise. 100 paise make 1 rupee.

MoneyRupees and paise

Q2. easy

How many paise make one rupee?

  1. A 100
  2. B 10
  3. C 50
  4. D 1000

Answer: A — 100

100 paise make 1 rupee. So ₹1 = 100 paise.

MoneyPaise

Q3. easy

Which symbol stands for the rupee?

  1. A ₹
  2. B $
  3. C £
  4. D %

Answer: A — ₹

The symbol ₹ stands for the Indian rupee. $ is dollar and £ is pound.

Q59. hard

Which overall statement about money is CORRECT?

  1. A Money is counted in rupees and paise (100 paise = ₹1); we add prices to find totals, subtract to find change, and multiply for many items at the same price
  2. B 1 rupee is 10 paise
  3. C Change is found by adding the price
  4. D Money cannot be added

Answer: A — Money is counted in rupees and paise (100 paise = ₹1); we add prices to find totals, subtract to find change, and multiply for many items at the same price

This sums up the topic: Indian money uses rupees (₹) and paise, with 100 paise in a rupee; we add to find total cost, subtract the cost from what we pay to get change, and multiply the price by the number of items.
