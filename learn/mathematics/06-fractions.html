Easy questions (5)

Q1. easy

A fraction shows a part of a:

  1. A Whole
  2. B Number line only
  3. C Table
  4. D Shape only

Answer: A — Whole

A fraction shows one or more equal parts of a whole thing or group. For example, half of a chapati is a fraction.

  1. A One half

Two equal parts means each part is one half, written as 1/2.

Q6. medium

  1. A Numerator
  2. B Denominator

Q58. hard

Which overall statement about fractions is CORRECT?

  1. A A fraction shows equal parts of a whole; the numerator (top) tells how many parts we take and the denominator (bottom) tells how many equal parts the whole has
  2. B Fractions use unequal parts
  3. C The top number tells the total parts
  4. D 1/4 is bigger than 1/2

Answer: A — A fraction shows equal parts of a whole; the numerator (top) tells how many parts we take and the denominator (bottom) tells how many equal parts the whole has

This sums up the topic: fractions like 1/2, 1/3 and 3/4 show equal parts of a whole; the numerator counts the parts taken and the denominator counts the total equal parts; and with the same top number, a bigger bottom number means a smaller piece.
