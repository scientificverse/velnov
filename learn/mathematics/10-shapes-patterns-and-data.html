Q59. hard

Which overall statement about shapes, patterns and data is CORRECT?

  1. A Flat (2D) shapes have sides and corners, solid (3D) shapes have faces and can roll or stack; patterns repeat or grow in a rule; and charts help us read and compare data
  2. B All shapes have 4 sides
  3. C A circle has 4 corners
  4. D Patterns never follow a rule

Answer: A — Flat (2D) shapes have sides and corners, solid (3D) shapes have faces and can roll or stack; patterns repeat or grow in a rule; and charts help us read and compare data

This sums up the topic: 2D shapes (circle, triangle, square, rectangle) have sides and corners; 3D shapes (cube, sphere, cone, cylinder, cuboid) have faces; patterns follow a rule of repeating or growing; and bar charts, tally marks and pictographs help us organise and compare information.
