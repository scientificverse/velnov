Q59. hard

Which overall statement about division is CORRECT?

  1. A Division shares a number into equal groups; it is the opposite of multiplication, a number divided by 1 stays the same, and by itself gives 1
  2. B Division always makes a bigger number
  3. C Any number divided by 0 gives that number
  4. D Division is the same as addition

Answer: A — Division shares a number into equal groups; it is the opposite of multiplication, a number divided by 1 stays the same, and by itself gives 1

This sums up the topic: division splits into equal groups or finds how many groups; it undoes multiplication (so we check by multiplying); dividing by 1 leaves the number unchanged, dividing a number by itself gives 1, and sometimes there is a remainder left over.
