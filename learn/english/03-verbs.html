Easy questions (4)

Q1. easy

A verb is a word that shows an:

  1. A Action or doing
  2. B Name
  3. C Colour
  4. D Place

Answer: A — Action or doing

A verb is a doing or action word. It tells us what someone or something does - run, jump, eat, sing.

VerbsAction word

Q2. easy

Which word is the verb in 'The boy runs fast'?

  1. A runs
  2. B boy
  3. C fast
  4. D the

Answer: A — runs

'Runs' is the action word - it tells us what the boy does, so it is the verb. 'Boy' is a noun and 'fast' tells how.

Q7. medium

Q56. hard

Which overall statement about verbs is CORRECT?

  1. A A verb shows an action or a state of being; helping verbs like is/am/are, was/were, has/have/had and do/does support the main verb
  2. B A verb only names a thing
  3. C Verbs never change form

This sums up the topic: verbs are doing or being words; they change with the subject (he sings, they sing) and tense (is/was), and helping verbs work with the main verb to build correct sentences.
