Easy questions (8)

Q1. easy

The three articles in English are:

  1. A a, an, the
  2. B is, am, are
  3. C he, she, it
  4. D and, but, or

Answer: A — a, an, the

'A', 'an' and 'the' are the three articles. They are little words placed before nouns.

Q2. easy

We use 'a' before a word that begins with a:

  1. A Consonant sound (b, c, d, ...)
  2. B Vowel sound
  3. C Capital letter
  4. D Number

Answer: A — Consonant sound (b, c, d, ...)

We use 'a' before words that begin with a consonant sound - a boy, a car, a dog, a table.

Q11. hard

Why do we say 'an hour' and not 'a hour'?

  1. A The 'h' in 'hour' is silent, so it starts with a vowel sound
  2. B 'Hour' is plural
  3. C 'Hour' is a special word
  4. D There is no reason

Answer: A — The 'h' in 'hour' is silent, so it starts with a vowel sound

The rule follows the SOUND, not the letter. In 'hour', the 'h' is silent and we hear a vowel sound ('our'), so we use 'an': 'an hour'.

Q12. hard

Why do we say 'a university' and not 'an university'?

  1. A 'University' begins with a 'y' consonant sound (yoo)
  2. B 'University' is plural
  3. C It is a mistake
  4. D 'University' begins with a vowel sound

Answer: A — 'University' begins with a 'y' consonant sound (yoo)

Although 'university' starts with the letter 'u', it sounds like 'yoo-niversity' - a consonant sound. So we use 'a': 'a university'.

Q16. hard

We use 'a' or 'an' when we talk about something for the FIRST time or in general. We use 'the' when:

  1. A We talk about a particular thing already known
  2. B The word is plural
  3. C The word is a verb
  4. D Never

Answer: A — We talk about a particular thing already known

'A/an' is used the first time or for any one thing; 'the' is used for a specific thing we already know. 'I saw a dog. The dog was barking.'

Q55. hard

Which overall statement about articles is CORRECT?

  1. A Use 'a' before consonant sounds, 'an' before vowel sounds, and 'the' for a particular or unique thing
  2. B Use 'a' before every word
  3. C Use 'the' only for people
  4. D Articles are never needed

Answer: A — Use 'a' before consonant sounds, 'an' before vowel sounds, and 'the' for a particular or unique thing

This sums up the topic: 'a' and 'an' are indefinite articles chosen by the SOUND that follows (consonant → a, vowel → an), while 'the' is the definite article for particular, known or unique things.
