Easy questions (5)

Q1. easy

A preposition is a word that shows the position or relation of a noun to another word, such as:

  1. A in, on, under, over
  2. B run, jump, sit
  3. C he, she, it
  4. D big, small

Answer: A — in, on, under, over

Prepositions like in, on, under, over, beside and between tell us where or when something is - 'The cat is under the table.'

Q58. hard

Which overall statement about prepositions is CORRECT?

  1. A A preposition shows the position, direction or time relation of a noun - words like in, on, under, over, between, at, since and for
  2. B A preposition is an action word
  3. C Prepositions name people
  4. D Prepositions describe verbs only

Answer: A — A preposition shows the position, direction or time relation of a noun - words like in, on, under, over, between, at, since and for

This sums up the topic: prepositions link a noun to the rest of the sentence, showing place (in, on, under), direction (into, across, through) and time (at, on, in, since, for).
