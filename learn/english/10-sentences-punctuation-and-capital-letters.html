Easy questions (6)

Q1. easy

A group of words that makes complete sense is called a:

  1. A Sentence
  2. B Letter
  3. C Sound
  4. D Syllable

Answer: A — Sentence

A sentence is a group of words that gives a complete meaning - 'The dog is barking.' A jumble of words without meaning is not a sentence.

Sentence

Q2. easy

Every sentence should begin with a:

  1. A Capital letter
  2. B Small letter
  3. C Full stop
  4. D Comma

Answer: A — Capital letter

The first word of every sentence starts with a capital letter - 'The sun is bright.'

Q57. hard

Which overall statement about sentences and punctuation is CORRECT?

  1. A A sentence begins with a capital letter and ends with a full stop, question mark or exclamation mark, depending on whether it tells, asks or exclaims
  2. B Sentences never need capital letters
  3. C All sentences end with a comma
  4. D Questions end with a full stop

Answer: A — A sentence begins with a capital letter and ends with a full stop, question mark or exclamation mark, depending on whether it tells, asks or exclaims

This sums up the topic: sentences start with a capital letter; statements and commands end with a full stop, questions with a question mark, and exclamations with an exclamation mark; commas separate items and set off names and 'yes/no'.
