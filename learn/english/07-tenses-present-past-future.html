Easy questions (4)

Q1. easy

The tense of a verb tells us the TIME of an action. The three main tenses are:

  1. A Present, past and future
  2. B Big, small and medium
  3. C Here, there and everywhere
  4. D A, an and the

Answer: A — Present, past and future

Tense shows when something happens: present (now), past (before) and future (later).

Tenses

Q2. easy

A verb that shows an action happening NOW or regularly is in the:

  1. A Present tense
  2. B Past tense
  3. C Future tense
  4. D No tense

Answer: A — Present tense

The present tense shows something happening now or done regularly - 'I eat', 'She plays every day'.

Present tense

Q3. easy

A verb that shows an action that has ALREADY happened is in the:

  1. A Past tense
  2. B Present tense
  3. C Future tense
  4. D No tense

Answer: A — Past tense

The past tense shows something that happened before now - 'I ate', 'She played yesterday'.

Past tense

Q4. easy

A verb that shows an action that WILL happen later is in the:

  1. A Future tense
  2. B Past tense
  3. C Present tense
  4. D No tense

Answer: A — Future tense

The future tense shows something that will happen - 'I will eat', 'She will play tomorrow'.

Future tense

Q58. hard

Which overall statement about tenses is CORRECT?

  1. A Tense shows the time of an action: present (now), past (already happened) and future (will happen); many verbs add '-ed' for the past, but some are irregular
  2. B Tense shows the colour of a noun
  3. C All verbs add '-ed' for the past
  4. D Tense never changes the verb

Answer: A — Tense shows the time of an action: present (now), past (already happened) and future (will happen); many verbs add '-ed' for the past, but some are irregular

This sums up the topic: the present shows now or habits, the past shows what already happened (regular verbs add '-ed', irregular ones change like go → went), and the future uses 'will'.
